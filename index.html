How a project runs.

A simple four-step flow, so you always know what’s happening and what’s next.

  1. Brief

    You tell me your goal, audience, deadline and budget. I confirm scope and price in writing.

  2. Plan

    I send a sitemap and page mockups for you to approve before I build anything.

  3. Create

    I design and code your site, and share a draft link so you can see progress.

  4. Deliver

    You get revisions, the live link and the files, plus clear next steps for keeping it running.

Questions clients ask.

Quick answers before you get in touch.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on scope. Send me your goal and budget, and I’ll reply with a clear price before any work begins.

How long does a website take?

A simple portfolio or landing page usually takes a few days. A store with a cart and several pages takes longer, and I’ll give you a date up front.

Can I hire you for just the design or just the build?

Yes. I can design a site for you, build a design you already have, or do both.

What do I need to give you?

Your logo, brand colours, text and photos if you have them. If not, I’ll help you plan what’s needed.

How do we communicate?

WhatsApp or email, whichever you prefer. I share drafts as links so feedback is quick.
